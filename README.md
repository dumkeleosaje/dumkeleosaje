**CS & AI Student @ University of Bath | Computational Systems Biology | TechBio**

I am a computer science and artificial intelligence student investigating the intersection of machine learning and human metabolic health. My core research interest is applying computational methods to understand **chronic hyperinsulinaemia** as a unified upstream driver of diseases currently treated as distinct clinical entities.

---

### 🧬 Summer 2026: The Metabolic AI Portfolio

I am currently building a three-part computational portfolio exploring metabolic dysfunction across different biological scales. 

**[→ View the full repository here](https://github.com/dumkeleosaje/metabolic-ai-portfolio)**

*   **Project A (Transcriptomic):** A machine learning classifier using LASSO feature selection, Random Forests, and UMAP to detect shared transcriptomic signatures across Type 2 Diabetes, PCOS, and NAFLD from NCBI GEO RNA-seq data.
*   **Project B (Pathway):** An NLP-driven knowledge graph (SciSpacy, NetworkX) extracting contested protein relationships from 400+ PubMed abstracts to map the boundary between autophagy and cellular senescence.
*   **Project C (Clinical/Systemic):** A state-space trajectory modeller using Gaussian Hidden Markov Models (HMM) on OhioT1DM continuous glucose monitor (CGM) data to infer moments of metabolic rigidity versus flexibility.
